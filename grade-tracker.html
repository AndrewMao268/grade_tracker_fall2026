"Current grade" = each category's weight × your average on what's graded in it, summed (ungraded items don't count toward it yet — standard method, matches Canvas-style calculators; a fully-ungraded category still counts its full weight in the denominator, which pulls this number down the more is left). "Grade so far" takes that same numerator but divides only by the weight of work that's actually been graded — i.e. "if the semester ended today using only what's graded, what % would that be." "Max possible" assumes 100% on every ungraded item. "Needed on remaining" shows the average you'd need across everything still ungraded, weighted by how much of your grade it's worth, to land at or above a cutoff — a dash means that cutoff is already out of reach even with a perfect score on the rest. MATH 212 uses Gwaltney's "best of 3 schemes" grading — this defaults to Scheme 1 weights (ME1 24% / ME2 24% / Final 32%, WebAssign 7.5% / Handwritten 7.5% / Quizzes 5%); edit the ME1/ME2/Final category weights once the Final happens if Scheme 2 (24/20/36) or 3 (20/20/40) ends up higher. Data lives only in this browser (not synced anywhere) — use Export below to back it up if you switch devices.
